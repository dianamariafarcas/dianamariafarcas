I am a Master's student in Cyber-Physical Systems at the Technical University of Cluj-Napoca (TUCN) with a background in automation, control systems, and software development. 
My Bachelor's thesis focused on controlling subprocesses of a pharmaceutical manufacturing line developed in MATLAB/Simulink, including a detailed analysis of the developed controllers for each subprocess considering different scenarios, which I also presented as a paper at the AQTR Student Forum 2026.

Building on this foundation, my current focus is on enhancing my skills and applying what I learned during my studies to practical applications. Now I am expanding my knowledge of Data and AI through dedicated trainings so I can integrate these technologies with control systems.

* 📍 Based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania
